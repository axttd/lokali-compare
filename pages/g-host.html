“I learned to paddle before I could ride a bicycle.”Pak Rahim, on growing up by the jetty

Get to know Pak Rahim

Where I grew up

In a stilt house 200 metres from the jetty. My family has fished this stretch of the Selangor River for three generations.

Why I started this

When the fireflies started to disappear, I wanted visitors to see them, and to pay to protect them.

Guests always ask me

“Do they ever switch off?” Yes, when it rains. I'll tell you the night before.

My favourite spot

The bend after the old jetty, where the river goes completely still.
